Event Details

Information for attendees of the BU AWM Math Symposium.

Symposium Overview

The BU AWM Math Symposium will be held on April 25th from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM in CAS B18 at Boston University. The event will feature lectures on math-related topics, followed by a brief lunch intermission, and then a Q&A session with professionals from both academia and industry.

The symposium is open to the BU community as well as students from nearby universities.

April 25th

Tentative Schedule

  • 9:00 – 10:00 AM — Guest arrival + coffee
  • 10:00 – 11:00 AM — Lecture on a Math Topic
  • 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM — Professional insights lecture
  • 12:00 – 1:00 PM — Lunch
  • 1:00 – 2:00 PM — Q&A panel with academia and industry professionals

Who Can Attend

We welcome students, faculty, and guests interested in mathematics, research, and professional paths in both academia and industry.

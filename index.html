About the Symposium

The symposium will be held on April 25th from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM in CAS B18. The event will feature two lectures on math-related topics, followed by a Q&A session with professionals from both academia and industry. It will be open to the BU community as well as students from nearby universities.

  • 10:00 – 11:00 AM: Lecture on Math Topic
  • 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Professional Insights Lecture
  • 12:00 – 1:00 PM: Lunch
  • 1:00 – 2:00 PM: Q&A Panel with Academia and Industry Professionals
